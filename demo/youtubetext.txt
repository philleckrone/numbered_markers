Numbered Markers is a Paratext tool that finds markers used in both their unnumbered and their numbered forms, such as \mt beside \mt1, or \li beside \li1. Both mean the same thing, but a project is easier to typeset and check when it uses one form. The tool shows where each form is used and changes the markers you tick, in the books you may edit. It adds a point to the project history and keeps a backup of each book it changes.

Download Install_Phils_tools.exe from the Paratext Plugins folder, and tick Numbered Markers in it:
